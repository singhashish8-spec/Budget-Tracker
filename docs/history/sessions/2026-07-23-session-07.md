## What happened

Opened with a batch of Phase-1/Phase-4 polish that could ship purely over
OTA: a hide-balances privacy blur, three-level animation control, a
shimmering skeleton loader, richer search, wider SMS coverage — explicitly
deferring the collapsing hero header and pull-to-refresh as needing a real
device to verify (#24). That deferral didn't last: the collapsing header was
requested next and took **three attempts across three PRs** to actually
work on-device. #25 built it with an `IntersectionObserver` and shipped as
1.0.31 — didn't appear on the phone at all. #26 root-caused it to a
`position: fixed` element nested inside a scroll container getting clipped,
fixed by portalling to `document.body` and switching to a plain scroll
check — still didn't show. #28 found the *actual* cause: both attempts
watched the *inner* scroll container, but this layout scrolls the *whole
page*, so a container-scoped observer never fires; switched to watching the
viewport directly (`root: null`) — this time it worked. In between, #27
added manual "check for updates" with a progress bar and reorganized
Settings into categories. #29 built the "Jul 2026 ▾" period dropdown on
Home. Then the "Big update" — a five-part rollout (#30–33, #36) of two new
primitives, `Collapse` (motion-aware expand/collapse) and `Sheet` (a
one-handed bottom sheet with a frozen header and thumb-zone-pinned actions)
— applied across every popup and list in the app: category popups, the
add-transaction sheet, budget/reminder/review popups, Home/Budget row
expand-in-place, smart-pattern expand, and collapsible date grouping on
Transactions. Independently, Google's **Jules** agent opened two PRs on its
own branches during this window: #34 (a `sql.js`/`jeep-sqlite` version
mismatch causing a WASM crash, plus CSV-injection hardening) and #37 (the
DB-reset-on-update bug, root-caused from Jules's side to a stale native
SQLite connection surviving a WebView reload). Neither was merged directly
— #38 closed out the session by describing an integration of Jules's
warranty-tracking, cooling-off-warning, and 50/50-split features, plus
reimplementing the same crash/injection/connection fixes Jules's own PRs
described. Also fixed mid-session: SMS-derived transactions couldn't be
deleted or cleaned up as duplicates, throwing a raw
`FOREIGN KEY constraint failed (code 787)` (#35) — see `findings.md`.
