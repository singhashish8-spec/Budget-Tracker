## What happened

Opened fixing two regressions from the previous session's long-press batch
(#56): a long-press timer that only cancelled on horizontal pointer
movement, so a straight-down scroll never cancelled it and the quick-action
menu's full-viewport overlay would pop mid-scroll and eat every further
touch — reported as "the scroll is not working after last update" — and a
swipe-actions layer that stretched over an expanded row's detail panel when
both gestures were active on the same row at once, visible in a
user-supplied screenshot (#59). Immediately after, a much bigger and
structural bug: **the entire app was unscrollable on every screen**,
root-caused to `.app-shell` using `min-height: 100vh` instead of a bounded
height — not a regression from any recent feature, but a latent defeat of
the app's whole scroll architecture that had been masked by an accidental
document-level scroll the whole time (#60) — see `findings.md` for the full
mechanism, including how the same bug also broke pull-to-refresh.

The session then turned to a direct owner report about the SMS parser:
reminder messages from banks and EMI companies were being registered as
real transactions, double-counting spend once at the reminder and again at
the actual debit. Root-caused to a substring check that couldn't
distinguish "debited" from "**will be** debited," plus a quieter bug where
templates leading with the account balance had that balance imported as the
spend. Rewrote `smsParse.js` with a completed-verb requirement, position-
scored amount extraction, and recurring-payment classification — verified
against the project's first automated test suite, 33 cases covering real
bank templates (#61) — see `findings.md`. Closed by adding a redacted
parser-feedback export: when a message is marked "not a transaction" from
inside the app, a stripped, non-identifying record of *why* the parser
misjudged it can be exported, closing the loop between "the parser got this
wrong" and "here's a corpus to fix it with" (#62).
