## What happened

Phase 1 of a "Pro roadmap": segmented EMI/bill progress bars, a cooling-off
warning that actually fires on any large single expense (not just when a
budget is nearly exhausted, which almost nobody hit), and a first-class
Warranty tracker with photo/PDF documents and a traffic-light status (#39).
On-device feedback came back fast: the floating top bar overlapped content
on *every* screen because each screen hardcoded a top padding that didn't
account for the safe-area inset on notched devices; the redundant camera
button (a second route to receipt scanning) was removed; warranties could
now be attached directly from a bill/EMI instead of re-entering them (#40).
Then a significant simplification: **the AI (Gemini) was removed
entirely** — it was the only part of the app that sent personal financial
documents to a third party, needed an API key almost nobody set, and could
fail or cost money at the worst moment. The one lost capability: importing
a bank-statement file or scanning a paper bill — both had gone through
Gemini. SMS auto-capture, being rule-based rather than AI, was untouched.
The same PR replaced edit-first card taps with read-only dashboards (a new
`DetentSheet` primitive) so opening a warranty/EMI/bill/subscription card to
*check* it didn't risk an accidental edit (#41). Group B of the roadmap
followed: side-hustle/GST tagging, straight-line net-worth projections,
manually-priced holdings (gold/SIP/FD/chit-fund/property), subscription
price-change alerts, budget-overspend forecasting, and opt-in envelope
(zero-based) budgeting (#42). A full-reset feature was added to Settings —
deliberately *not* just the existing reset function, because that left
auto-backup snapshots on disk, which the very next launch would offer right
back (#43). The session's biggest native-only drop followed: Android share
sheet integration for filing a shared PDF/photo straight into Warranties,
local notifications for bills/warranties with rebuild-on-every-launch
(because Android can silently drop scheduled alarms), tiered haptics
(iOS-style — respond to state changes, not every touch), `FLAG_SECURE` tied
to hide-balances, and launcher shortcuts (#44). Closed with two same-day,
on-device-reported bug fixes: the app hanging forever on "Restoring your
data" after a reinstall — root-caused to a swallowed restore error leaving
the loading overlay stuck with no way past, fixed with a real failure path,
a 45-second deadline, and per-row isolation in `importBackup` so one bad
document can't sink the whole restore (#45); and haptics firing nothing on
the most-tapped surfaces — root-caused to `Haptics.selectionChanged()` being
a no-op on Android unless `selectionStart()` had run first, which nothing
ever called (#46).

## What the owner asked/reported

| What was asked/reported | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Top bar overlapped content on every screen | Root-caused to hardcoded padding not accounting for the safe-area inset; fixed across all 13 screens (#40) |
| (reported after 1.3.0 reinstall) app stuck forever on "Restoring your data" | Root-caused to a swallowed restore error; added a real failure path, a timeout, and per-document restore isolation (#45) |
| (reported after 1.3.1) no haptics felt at all | Root-caused to `selectionChanged()` requiring an uncalled `selectionStart()`; switched most-tapped surfaces to `impact(Light)` (#46) |
